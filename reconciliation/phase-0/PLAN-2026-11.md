**Question framework.** The current question types (`meta-research`, `experimentation`,
`simulation`, `engineering-decision`, `discussion`, `analysis`) have no place for a programme
bet: a question whose answer is a recommended position with its trade-offs and the conditions
under which it should be revisited. To do: either add a type (working name `programme-trade`)
or extend `engineering-decision` with fields for options, the trade axes, the recommended bet
and its revisit trigger; then register 1.1–1.5 as research questions (1.1 extends rq-0-84,
1.2 and 1.3 extend rq-0-52, 1.4 extends rq-0-87, 1.5 extends rq-0-58).

1.5 is the question the smelting work (section 2.0) needs most: the sulfur limit decides
which process routes are even candidates.
