### 1. Evidence
- **OSIRIS-REx Bennu Sample Return (2023/2024)**: Confirmed CI-chondrite composition with >10% water in hydrated phyllosilicates. Ground truth for target mineralogy, but no extraction performed.
- **Sercel et al. NIAC (2019-2022)**: Demonstrated "Optical Mining" thermal extraction on CI simulant in a vacuum chamber at ~8 kW scale. Extracted water from simulant, but microgravity handling and continuous throughput were not demonstrated.
- **Metzger et al. (2023)**: Physics-based thermal extraction model (axisymmetric Crank-Nicholson) for volatile extraction from regolith. Validated against lab data but remains an analytical model.

### 2. Current TRL
**TRL 3–4**. Analytical and experimental proof-of-concept for thermal extraction of water from simulants exists (TRL 3). Component-level lab vacuum tests have been performed (TRL 4 for specific subsystems), but integrated breadboard validation of continuous excavation, heating, and vapor capture in a relevant environment (microgravity, abrasive regolith) is incomplete. The unmet exit criterion for TRL 5 is documented test performance of a breadboard in a relevant environment with defined scaling requirements.

### 3. Target and Difficulty
**Target**: TRL 6 by KDP-0B.
**AD²**: 6. Several competing approaches exist (mechanical excavation + enclosed heating vs. optical mining/direct solar concentration), and the path to managing abrasive, cohesive regolith while capturing vapor in microgravity without massive volatile loss is not clear.

### 4. Path
1. **Sample Validation**: Lab extraction from actual Bennu/Ryugu samples to validate simulant thermal kinetics.
2. **Subsystem TVAC**: 10+ kg scale thermal vacuum extraction comparing optical vs. mechanical heating.
3. **Microgravity Handling**: Parabolic flight tests of the regolith-to-heater transfer mechanism to quantify volatile loss.
4. **0A Pathfinder**: In-space demonstrator extracting ~1-10 kg of water from a NEA.

### 5. Risk and Fallback
**Risk**: Architecture-change. If the water pilot fails or proves economically unviable, the architecture must abandon water EP and ISPP, falling back to imported Earth propellants (Iodine/Argon) for tugs.
**Fallback**: Source all propellant from Earth and abandon the water pilot, relying solely on dry metallic beneficiation for Phase 1.

### 6. Where others might disagree
The assertion that optical mining and mechanical excavation are at TRL 3-4 for this specific architecture. Proponents of optical mining may argue TRL 4-5 based on NIAC Phase III lab demonstrations, but those tests do not address the continuous 10-100 t/yr throughput and microgravity vapor capture required here.
