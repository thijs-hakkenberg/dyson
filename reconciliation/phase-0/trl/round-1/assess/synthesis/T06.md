## 2. Where they agree

- **Current TRL.** All three rate the required system TRL 2–3 and treat terrestrial carbonyl refining as mature in its own environment but not transferable. Claude calls it TRL 9 in that environment; GPT calls it mature terrestrial technology.
- **TRL 2 is met.** All three accept the documented application of established chemistry.
- **TRL 4 is unmet.** All three say no integrated closed-loop breadboard exists and no relevant environment has been defined.
- **No gravity-relevant demonstration exists.** None of the three cites any carbonyl step demonstrated in microgravity or vacuum ambient.
- **"No melt" does not mean "gravity-independent."** All three say solids handling, separation and collection are unproven in low gravity:
  - Claude lists at least five gravity-dependent heritage unit operations.
  - GPT lists six system functions still to demonstrate.
  - Gemini cites multiphase flow and dust containment.
- **Toxic-gas containment is a driving requirement**, even though the plant is uncrewed.
- **AD² 6.** All three base this on competing, unresolved contacting and handling approaches.
- **The first decisive step is representative-feed chemistry** at gram-to-kg scale, costing roughly $2–8M over 1–2 years (all three).
- **Fallback.** The rotating smelting furnace appears in all three fallback chains.
