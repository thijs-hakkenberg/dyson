## 2. Where the models agree

**On what the evidence shows (all three):**
- The Bennu (and, for Claude and GPT, Ryugu) returned samples are evidence about the **resource**. They are not evidence of extraction.
- Microgravity feed handling, containment and vapour capture have **not been demonstrated**. Continuous throughput at pilot scale has not been demonstrated either.
- Mechanical excavation with enclosed heating and optical mining are **competing approaches with no demonstrated winner**.
- A thermal-vacuum bench extractor in the tens-of-kg class, comparing the two routes, is the next major ground step. The stated cost is $5–20M (Claude, GPT) or $5–15M (Gemini).
- Fallback: Fe-Ni production proceeds. Any water needed comes from Earth, and the tugs remain on imported propellant. Gemini frames this as abandoning water EP and ISPP; Claude and GPT frame it as the iodine/argon reference already being the baseline (D-05).

**Claude and GPT agree (Gemini does not address these points):**
- The rating boundary excludes electrolysis, LOX/LH₂ and thrusters. Rq-0-33 cannot raise this TRL.
- The feed is water bound in hydrated minerals. Free ice is not assumed.
- Hydration or mineralogical water content is not the same as recoverable yield.
- A C-type pilot is probably on a **separate site** from the Fe-Ni plant, unless prospecting shows otherwise.
- The **pilot purpose and product purity specification are undefined**, and this blocks a defined relevant environment.
- The Paper 05 Monte Carlo, other design studies and panel decisions earn **no TRL credit**.
- **Lunar polar water is not a meaningful fallback** under the revised architecture.
- Short parabolic or drop-tower tests are too short to validate continuous feed, heat-up, capture and restart.
- No 0B baseline element depends on water. The two models then classify the risk differently (see §3.3).

## 3. Where the models diverge, and why

### 3.1 Current TRL: Claude 3–4 and Gemini 3–4, versus GPT 2–3 (integrated)

| Model | Basis |
|---|---|
| **Claude** | TRL 3 is met because Garenne et al. 2014 TGA validates the key parameter (water release versus temperature) on real meteorites. Simulant work (Britt 2019) and small vacuum demos (Honeybee WINE, Sercel APIS) support this, though Claude marks both demos as not confident. The upper bound of 4 holds only if those demos are accepted as breadboard validation of extraction alone. |
| **Gemini** | Sercel NIAC optical mining (marked confident: ~8 kW, ~1–10 kg batches). Metzger et al. 2023 is described as "validated against lab data". |
| **GPT** | TRL 3 requires validated predictions of release versus temperature and time, energy per kg, **capture fraction and contaminant loading** on representative feed. GPT has not established these test records. The optical-mining figures are unverified. |

**Root of the divergence.** The models apply different definitions of the TRL 3 "key parameters":
- Claude treats release versus temperature as sufficient.
- GPT requires capture and contaminant data as well.

The models also give different credence to the same optical-mining record. Claude's integrated-chain rating ("3 at best") and GPT's (2–3) overlap more than the headline ranges suggest.

**What would settle it:**
1. A panel ruling on which parameters count as "key" for the TRL 3 exit of this chain.
2. Retrieval of the Sercel/APIS and Honeybee WINE test reports. These would need to show measured water captured, run duration, power, and agreement with prior predictions.
3. Verification of the Metzger reference: authorship, arXiv ID, and whether it was validated against test data.

### 3.2 Target TRL at KDP-0B: Claude 5, versus Gemini and GPT 6

| Model | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **Claude** | Under D-02 the pilot is not project-ending, so the internal-milestone band of 4–5 applies. Claude sets the top of that band because KDP-0B commits a flight pilot. The pilot itself then serves as the TRL 6–7 demonstration. |
| **GPT** | TRL 6 is required *before* committing to the production pilot. |
| **Gemini** | TRL 6, with no stated derivation. |

**Nature of the divergence.** This is an interpretation of programme policy, not a disagreement about evidence. AD² follows from it: Claude rates AD² 6 for a step to TRL 6, which matches the other two models.

**What would settle it:**
- A programme ruling on how D-02 applies to a pilot that is itself a flight demonstration.
- The outcome of rq-0-55. Claude's escalation clause raises the target to 6 if water EP is selected.

### 3.3 Risk class: Claude cost-increase, versus Gemini and GPT architecture-change

- **Claude and GPT share the factual premise** that no 0B baseline element depends on water. They label the consequence differently:
  - Claude: the loss is option value, so the class is cost-increase.
  - GPT: failure closes or defers the water option, which counts as an architecture change.
- **Gemini's premise differs.** Gemini states that failure would force the architecture to "abandon water EP and ISPP". Claude and GPT treat neither as part of the 0B baseline.

**What would settle it:**
- The rq-0-55 outcome. Claude moves to architecture-change if water EP enters Phase 1 transport.
- A programme definition of whether closing an optional element counts as an architecture change.

### 3.4 Years to target: 3–6, 5–8 and 6–10

These figures follow from the target (TRL 5 versus 6) and from path content:
- GPT includes full-rate ground endurance and an orbital prototype as required steps.
- Claude makes the equivalents conditional or optional.

Resolving §3.2 largely resolves this.

### 3.5 Yield and feed assumptions

| Model | Recoverable water | Recovery factor | Feed mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude | 4–12 wt% | (included in yield) | ~80–2,500 t/yr |
| GPT | 5–15 wt% | 50–80% | 83–4,000 t/yr envelope; 143–1,429 t/yr reference case |
| Gemini | ">10% water" in Bennu, stated as confirmed | — | not derived |

- Claude and GPT both label their figures as assumptions.
- Claude notes that feed mass, power and tailings move by about 3× on the yield assumption alone.

**What would settle it:**
- Evolved-gas and TGA data on returned Bennu and Ryugu material that distinguish recoverable water from total water, with co-released species measured.
- The sampled mineralogy of the selected target, through the D-02 target gate.
