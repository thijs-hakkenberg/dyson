# Consolidated rating: T23, native Fe-Ni production and consolidation in rotating modules (GPT route)

**Rapporteur's note.** This consolidates three assessments: Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT-6.1 Sol. All three rate the same route:

- magnetic beneficiation of native Fe-Ni;
- consolidation at 0.05–0.15 g in a rotating module;
- rotation treated as an experimentally gated hypothesis.

The models do not draw the subsystem boundary the same way:

- **Claude** includes beneficiation within the rated technology.
- **GPT** treats qualified concentrate as the input, so beneficiation is an upstream dependency.
- **Gemini** names beneficiation in its route description but assesses consolidation only.

Claude and GPT both state that the March 2026 entry (TRL 3–4, hybrid-gravity smelting plus Si zone refining) assessed a different technology and does not transfer. Gemini does not address that entry. I add no technical claims of my own. Arithmetic on figures the models supplied is labelled as such.

## 2. Where the models agree

- **TRL 3 is not reached.** All three find no documented results validating key parameters in the 0.05–0.15 g regime. The 2–3 versus 2–2 difference is a reporting difference about what an upper bound means. It is not a disagreement about whether the TRL 3 criterion is met.
- **No evidence exists in the required environment.** No model identifies any sustained metallurgical or consolidation experiment at 0.05–0.15 g.
- **ISS electromagnetic levitation (EML) does not transfer.** All cite ISS-EML as gram-scale, containerless and at 0 g. All agree it does not demonstrate partial-g, dirty-feed or production-rate processing.
- **Terrestrial heritage does not bridge the g gap.** All treat terrestrial melting, casting and consolidation as establishing the physics at 1 g only.
- **AD² is 6.** The common rationale is that competing routes exist and the path is not selected.
- **Target is TRL 6 by KDP-0B, risk class architecture-change.** Primary fallback is a no-melt route (the Claude/rq-0-56 route).
- **Credit withheld from D'Angelo et al.** Both Claude and GPT decline to credit arXiv 2102.09815 as unverified. Gemini does not cite it.
- **Rotation may be unnecessary.** Claude and GPT both state the route must allow for solid-state or confined processing to make rotation unnecessary. Both place a cheap ground gate before flight spending.
- **Escalation if every route fails.** Claude and GPT both invoke the D-02 fallback "0A only, Earth supply". Gemini does not address this case.
