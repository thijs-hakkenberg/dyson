## 3. Where the panel diverges, and why

### 3.1 Current TRL: GPT 2–3, Claude 2–4, Gemini 3–4

**What each model relies on:**
- **Claude** credits TRL 3 for the tug on piecewise validation. Its evidence is flight blankets at 20–30 kW, III-V degradation versus displacement damage dose, and deep-space SEP operation. It caps the site at 2 because there is no dust, anchoring or kV data.
- **Gemini** credits TRL 3 on component-level proof of concept. Its evidence includes 50 kW Gateway PPE arrays and ThrustMe iodine work.
- **GPT** makes TRL 3 *conditional*. It requires a documented, test-correlated scaling analysis linking heritage experiments to the proposed modular MW design, and states that none is supplied.

**Unmet exit criterion.** All three agree that TRL 4 needs:
- a documented relevant environment; and
- a breadboard whose performance agrees with predictions.

**What would settle it.** A panel review of whether any existing document correlates ROSA/iROSA/Psyche test data with a candidate MW module layout, which is GPT's criterion. Producing that document is step 1 in both Claude's and GPT's paths. If it exists and holds up, TRL 3 is supportable for the tug. If not, GPT's TRL 2 floor stands.

### 3.2 Can TRL 6 be reached on the ground?

**Positions:**
- **Gemini** reaches TRL 6 with ground TVAC of a full or half-scale (500 kW) quadrant plus high-fidelity simulation.
- **Claude** says ground vacuum, thermal and plume tests reach TRL 5 at most. Its reasons:
  - a full-scale MW ground test is physically impossible;
  - the Space Power Facility (~30 × 37 m), which Claude cites alone, cannot host it.
- **GPT** says gravity offloading alone cannot close microgravity dynamics, so TRL 6 needs a flight prototype.

**What would settle it.** Two things:
1. Agree the TRL 6 acceptance basis before testing, as GPT recommends.
2. Compare gravity-offloaded ground modal results against on-orbit modal data for the same hardware (e.g. ROSA/iROSA flight dynamics data, which Claude proposes in its step 5). That comparison shows whether ground-correlated models can be trusted at larger scale.

### 3.3 Flight-demo scale and vehicle

**Positions:**
- **Claude:** ≥200–500 kW multi-wing demo (~1/5–1/40 of a tug) on "the 0A pathfinder", at $150–500M.
- **GPT:** 1–2.5 MW modular flight prototype at $400M–1.5B. GPT warns it "should not be silently inserted into the 200–600 kg scout class" and expects a separate demonstrator to be needed. GPT also says a full 8 MW site flight need not be mandatory if repeat units and aggregation effects are validated.

Claude's own text says 0A scouts at 200–600 kg use existing kW arrays. It is therefore unclear whether Claude's "0A pathfinder" is a different, larger vehicle; Claude should clarify.

**What would settle it.** Two things:
1. Confirm the 0A pathfinder's mass and power envelope.
2. Decide, on the same acceptance basis as §3.2, whether a subscale flight plus a validated scaling model is acceptable for TRL 6.

### 3.4 Risk class

**Positions:**
- **Claude and Gemini (YAML):** architecture-change.
- **GPT:** project-ending for the selected solar 0B architecture.

All three describe the same causal chain: degraded fallbacks raise delivered $/kg, which may fail the D-02 economics gate. They disagree on how to label it.

**What would settle it.** Run the D-02 economics model with the fallback configuration (~0.3–0.5 MWe tugs, larger fleet, longer trips, reduced site power). If it closes, the risk is architecture-change. If it does not, the risk is project-ending.

### 3.6 Sizing distance and resulting area

- **Claude** sizes at the maximum heliocentric distance, bracketed 1.3–1.7 AU. This gives a tug area of ~6,400–27,000 m² (1 AU BOL basis, ~330 W/m²).
- **GPT** uses 0.9–1.3 AU as the stated envelope. It requires sizing at the actual maximum powered distance or explicit derating. At 1.3 AU (~156 W/m² net EOL, assumed factors) this gives a tug area of ~6,400–16,000 m².
- **Site area:** both give ~16,000–51,000 m².
- **Gemini** does not size by distance.

The tug upper-bound difference comes from Claude's 1.7 AU high case. Claude notes that fixing 1.2 AU would cut area by ~2×. GPT notes that 1.3 versus 1.2 AU adds ~17% area.

**What would settle it.** Select the trajectories and fix the maximum powered distance (step 1 for both Claude and GPT).
