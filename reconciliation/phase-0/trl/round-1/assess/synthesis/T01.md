## 2. Where the panel agrees

- **The prior rating is overstated.** No model supports the March 2026 entry of TRL 6–7.
  - Claude and GPT both state that AEPS has not flown or achieved mission operations.
  - Claude and GPT both state that magnetic shielding does not remove other failure modes, naming pole-cover, cathode, feed, PPU and cluster interaction.
  - Gemini anticipates the xenon-heritage argument for TRL 5–6 and rejects it.
- **The lower bound of TRL 2 is met** by documented concepts, per all three.
- **TRL 6 at KDP-0B with no waivers** is the target in all three.
- **Overall AD² is 6** in all three.
- **Risk class is project-ending for the 0B transport chain** in all three, with D-02 "0A only, Earth supply" as the terminal fallback. Claude and GPT both qualify this: single-branch failure is an architecture change.
- **Branch-level AD² ordering:** where models give branch values (Claude, GPT), the order is Xe easiest (4–5), Ar intermediate (5 or 5–6), I₂ hardest (6 or 6–7).
- **Required life is undemonstrated at relevant power in every branch.**
  - The best wear evidence at Hall-thruster power levels cited by Claude and GPT is ~3,570 h at 12.5 kW Xe.
  - The best throughput evidence cited by the same two is ~918 kg at ≤7 kW Xe (NEXT, gridded ion).
- **Xenon heritage does not transfer to iodine or argon.** Each model states this in some form.
- **The rating must cover the integrated system, not the thruster alone:** PPUs, feed, cathodes, cluster interactions, plume deposition on arrays, and the heat path to the bus. Claude and GPT state this explicitly; Gemini lists PPU and cluster as non-existent at this scale.
- **The radiator/bus heat load from the thruster must be measured.** Claude and GPT both say neither of the bounding thermal cases can be assumed.
- **Related RQs** are the same set in all three: rq-0-16, -19, -20, -53, -54.
