# TRL assessment: T11 Rendezvous scout spacecraft (with subsurface sampling per Gemini/GPT)

## What is being rated

The March 2026 entry `autonomous-asteroid-prospecting` described a different system: a 50-unit constellation of 80–120 kg survey craft doing remote spectroscopy at more than 20 targets per craft per year. That system is superseded. T11 is now:

- **Scale:** 4–10 scouts of 200–600 kg using solar-electric propulsion (SEP). Each rendezvouses with 1–3 NEAs, subject to trajectory and reuse validation per GPT.
- **Function A, rendezvous and remote characterisation:** spectra, imaging, shape, gravity and bulk density, and possibly gamma-ray/neutron (GRS) and radar. All three models include this.
- **Function B, subsurface sampling for bulk grade:** Gemini and GPT include this. It must yield **recoverable** Fe-Ni grade, meaning grade after beneficiation, not bulk Fe (rq-0-50). The D-02 target gate requires a *sampled* grade, sampled at depth on at least 2 candidates.

The two functions are at very different maturity, so I rate them separately. The system rating is set by the weakest function on the critical path.

### Assumptions I am stating, because the requirement does not fix them

| Parameter | Assumption | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sampling depth | ~0.3–2 m, below the gardened and thermally processed layer, at more than one site per body | Rubble-pile mixing means a single surface point is not representative |
| Representative sample mass | ~0.1–10 kg per target | Native metal occurs as discrete grains (sub-mm to mm) at 2–20 wt%. Sampling theory (Gy-type) puts the mass needed for roughly ±20% relative grade precision in this range. **Not yet derived for this project**; this is rq-0-50 direction 3. |
| Assay | In-situ: magnetic separation plus mass or XRF/GRS of the magnetic fraction. Alternative: Earth return. | Sample return of kg-scale mass from 1–3 targets is unlikely to fit 200–600 kg |
| Environment | Deep space at 0.9–1.3 AU; NEA surface gravity ~10⁻⁵–10⁻⁴ m/s²; cohesion ~0.1 Pa to kPa (crust); charged abrasive fines; 4–8 yr radiation dose | Per the rating rules |
| Mission duration | 4–8 yr per scout, covering cruise plus 1–3 rendezvous | Derived from 1–3 targets with SEP |

**Corrections to the March entry and the supplied RQs:**

- **NEA Scout is not heritage.** It launched on Artemis I (2022) but contact was never established, so it demonstrated nothing at an asteroid.
- **AstroForge Odin (2025)** also failed early. Commercial small-craft NEA missions have, so far, no successful rendezvous.
- **The rq-0-2 and rq-0-3 Monte Carlo results are design studies, not evidence.** These are the 47% vs 100% survey efficiency result and the 38% high-value ceiling. rq-0-3 also uses simplified Hohmann Δv on a synthetic population and is not usable for the 1–3-target question.
- **Citations I have not verified.** Several arXiv IDs and attributions in the supplied literature reviews are unverified by me. Treat them as unverified until checked:
  - the DISCUS NIR spectrometer specification (I understood DISCUS as a radar concept);
  - "Ballouz et al. 2020" for the TAG bearing capacity;
  - the "sank ~50 cm / 8 m crater" figures.

  The OSIRIS-REx finding of an extremely weak, fluid-like near-surface is real; Lauretta et al. and Walsh et al. reported it in 2022. The precise numbers should be taken from those papers.

## 3. Target and difficulty

**What D-02 implies:**

- Scouts are a 0A asset, but their output is the **target gate** at KDP-0B.
- The gate cannot be passed without sampled grade, and project-ending items get no waivers. The sampling function is therefore effectively project-ending for 0B *as the gate is currently worded*.
- Hardware must be TRL 6 by scout PDR, which is normal NASA practice.
- Grade data only exists once the scouts have *flown and sampled*. The sampling function must therefore reach **TRL 7 in operation on at least 2 candidates before KDP-0B**.

**Needed by:**

- **TRL 6** at scout PDR, roughly 0A year 2–3 (~2028–2029).
- **TRL 7** with sampled grade from at least 2 targets before KDP-0B.

With 3–5 yr development, 1–3 yr cruise and months of proximity operations, the first sampled grade arrives at the **earliest around 2032–2035**. KDP-0B cannot credibly sit earlier than that, and this should be stated in the 0A schedule.

**AD², using the project's working anchors:**

| Step | AD² | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Function A, 6→7, multi-target at 200–600 kg on xenon | **3–4** | New development within the experience base; Dawn and Hayabusa2 are close analogues |
| Function B, 3→6/7 | **6** | Several competing approaches remain and the path is not clear: impactor plus touchdown, anchored drill, penetrator, sample return vs in-situ assay. There is negative heritage on anchoring and penetration (Philae). The representative-mass and depth requirement is undefined. AD² **5** is justified only after a downselect to one route with a defined assay. |

**Years to target:** Function B reaches TRL 6 in 4–7 yr. TRL 7 by first-flight sampling comes at 6–10 yr.

## 5. Risk and fallback

**Risk class: architecture-change.** It becomes project-ending for 0B only if the target gate wording, "sampled grade", is held fixed with no alternative route.

If depth sampling and assay do not reach TRL 6 by scout PDR, the options are, in order:

1. **Fly remote-only scouts.** Use spectra, GRS/neutron, gravity-derived density vs grain density, and radar, then re-word the target gate to a remote-inferred grade with an explicit uncertainty band. This depends on step 2 showing that the band is acceptable.
2. **Make the 0A pathfinder demonstrator the grade certifier** at the single leading candidate, by excavating and beneficiating. This costs one target, one cycle and several years.
3. **Fall back to "0A only, Earth supply"**, as pre-committed in D-02.

## 6. Where others might disagree

- **Whether sampling is needed at all.** Claude's configuration holds that GRS plus density plus spectra can bound native metal well enough. GPT holds that remote data does not certify bulk grade. I side with GPT, because GRS measures total Fe and cannot separate native from oxidised Fe. However, step 2 is a cheap empirical test that should settle this before hardware money is spent. If Claude is right, T11 is TRL 6–7 and is not critical.
- **Crediting Hayabusa2.** Some assessors would rate subsurface sampling TRL 5–6 on SCI/TD2 heritage. I don't, because:
  - the heritage returned grams to Earth and never assayed grade;
  - the required function is in-situ, kg-scale and recoverable-grade;
  - scale ratios are about 20–2,000× in mass.
- **The representative sample mass.** My 0.1–10 kg is an assumption, not a derivation. If a metal-rich (M/CB-like) target were chosen, much smaller samples might suffice, which would ease Function B.
- **1–3 targets per 200–600 kg craft.** This is undemonstrated; Dawn did two targets at about twice the mass. GPT's caveat stands, and three targets may not close with sampling hardware on board.
- **Iodine commonality.** Adopting the D-05 propellant on the scouts would pull SEP from TRL 6–7 down to TRL 4–5 for no Phase 0A benefit. I would keep xenon on the scouts.
