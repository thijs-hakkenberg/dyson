## Milky Way panorama

`milkyway_eso0932a.webp` — "The Milky Way panorama" (eso0932a), **ESO/S. Brunier**,
https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/ — licensed under CC BY 4.0.
Changes: resized to 4096×2048 and converted to WebP. The image is an equirectangular
projection in galactic coordinates (galactic centre at the image centre); the explorer
rotates it into the ecliptic J2000 frame.
