## Background

All three models exclude programme management, systems engineering and mission assurance (PM/SE/MA) from bom-0-7. No other line claims it, and Claude rates it the largest unowned scope risk. Separately, operations prices 0A at about 4–5.5 years, while the D-02 KDP-0B window puts it at 6–15 (CI-27).

## Why This Matters

PM/SE/MA is typically 10–15% of a space programme, so leaving it out biases every total low. The 0A length drives the run-rate lines and the A4 check (rq-0-84).

## How this gets answered

- **Engineering decision**: A WBS dictionary entry for PM/SE/MA with a cost basis (percentage of development or a staffing plan), and an approved planning length for 0A that the run-rate lines use.

## Research Directions

1. Placement options: a new line, inside bom-0-11, or split across items.
2. Cost basis from comparable programmes.
3. A planning 0A length, consistent with rq-0-63.
