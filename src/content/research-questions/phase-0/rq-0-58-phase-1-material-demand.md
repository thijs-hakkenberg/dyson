## Background

No Phase 0 document ties output to Phase 1 need (finding P0-F19). Claude: ISRU-eligible Phase 1 demand is 0.04–11 kt/yr, unknown within 65×. Gemini proposes 1,200 t/yr structural Fe-Ni plus 500 t/yr water to SEL1; GPT 0.6–1.0 kt/yr Fe-Ni and zero Al, Si or water. All three want a signed schedule before committing to 0B.

Raised by the Phase 0 re-assessment (round 2, 2026-10-12): `reconciliation/phase-0/round-2/reconciliation/phase.md` and `reconciliation/phase-0/decisions.md`.

## Why This Matters

The demand gate in D-02 and the go/scale decision for 0B in D-01 both hinge on it. ACH found it the most discriminating, and cheapest, information.

## How this gets answered

- **Analysis** — Mass, material, purity, delivery point and date for every Phase 1 item that could use Phase 0 Fe-Ni; requires the Phase 1 re-assessment.

1. Demand schedule known within 3× (Claude G6).
