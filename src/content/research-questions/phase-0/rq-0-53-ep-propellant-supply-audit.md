## Background

Tug propellant demand is 150–1,200 t/yr. The BOM says "krypton/iodine (xenon not viable: demand exceeds global production 15–20×)". The panel disputes the basis: GPT calls the 15–20× claim unsupported; krypton supply is "poorly documented" (Claude 10²–10³ t/yr, Gemini 150–180 t/yr); iodine is ~3×10⁴ t/yr.

Raised by the Phase 0 re-assessment (round 2, 2026-10-12): `reconciliation/phase-0/round-2/reconciliation/phase.md` and `reconciliation/phase-0/decisions.md`.

## Why This Matters

D-05 keeps the tug propellant-agnostic until supply is documented. A propellant the market cannot supply at this rate cannot be the baseline.

## How this gets answered

- **Literature research** — Dated production and price data (USGS, industry gas suppliers, iodine producers) for each propellant; competing demand (satellite constellations); price elasticity.

## Research Directions

1. Annual production, reserves and price trend per propellant.
2. Share of world supply Phase 0 would take, at 150, 600 and 1,200 t/yr.
3. Supply-contract feasibility.
