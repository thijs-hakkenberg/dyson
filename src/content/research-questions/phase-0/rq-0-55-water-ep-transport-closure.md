## Background

Water EP is the only route by which ISPP gets a Phase 0 customer (Claude). Gemini proposes 1,500 t/yr of industrial water at Isp 800–1,200 s. GPT: water at 200–800 s "does not create self-sustaining transport closure"; the mass ratio is ~21 at 200 s for 6 km/s.

Raised by the Phase 0 re-assessment (round 2, 2026-10-12): `reconciliation/phase-0/round-2/reconciliation/phase.md` and `reconciliation/phase-0/decisions.md`.

## Why This Matters

Decides whether a water-EP qualification track ($0.2–0.6B, Claude) is worth funding, and whether bom-0-6 survives in Phase 0.

## How this gets answered

- **Analysis** — Vehicle mass-ratio and fleet closure at Isp 200–1,200 s, with water from a C-type site vs imported.
- **Literature research** — State of water/steam and water-plasma thrusters at high power.
