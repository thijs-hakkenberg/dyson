## Background

All three models move robot power to a 1–3 kV tether from site power. Gemini alone raises insulation life on abrasive, electrostatically charged regolith.

Raised by the Phase 0 re-assessment (round 2, 2026-10-12): `reconciliation/phase-0/round-2/reconciliation/phase.md` and `reconciliation/phase-0/decisions.md`.

## Why This Matters

The tethered-robot architecture depends on it; failure modes are arcing and abrasion.

## How this gets answered

- **Experiment** — Abrasion and vacuum-arc testing of candidate tether insulation with regolith simulant, thermal cycling and charging.
- **Literature research** — Lunar dust and HV cable literature (Apollo, Artemis surface power).

## Research Directions

1. Insulation wear rate and arc thresholds.
2. Tether management in microgravity.
