## Background

Launch cost is the main cost spread between the models ($3–96B), and much of it is a basis difference: Claude and Gemini price per kg to LEO ($1,200–4,000 today, $300–1,000 Starship-mature), GPT per kg delivered to the operating network ($3,000–12,000), noting LEO prices "do not establish this deep-space delivered price".

Raised by the Phase 0 re-assessment (round 2, 2026-10-12): `reconciliation/phase-0/round-2/reconciliation/phase.md` and `reconciliation/phase-0/decisions.md`.

## Why This Matters

Delivered price is the sensitivity point that can flip D-01 (falsifier: sustained < $1,000/kg to LEO), and the denominator of the Earth-supply comparison (rq-0-61).

## How this gets answered

- **Literature research** — Current and announced launch and in-space transfer prices; request destination-specific quotations (LEO → SEL1, LEO → NEA).

## Research Directions

1. One delivered-price basis for all cost lines.
2. Price trajectory scenarios to 2040.
