## Background

The framework-mode deliberations on the WP-A gate rulings (rq-0-65 … rq-0-75) stopped after one round with three positions on several rules that established practice may already settle. The models argued from first principles and partial recollection of NASA, DoD, ESA and GAO guidance; none was given that guidance. The owner has confirmed the converged points (`reconciliation/phase-0/trl/gate-rulings.yaml`) and asked that the rest be answered from evidence rather than ruled by intuition.

Feeds the decision sheet (`reconciliation/phase-0/trl/gate-rulings-sheet.md`) items 2.1, 2.12, 2.14, 2.16, 2.17 and 2.18.

## Why This Matters

Partners and reviewers will read the gates in agency vocabulary. A rule that departs from practice needs a reason; one that follows practice needs the citation.

## How this gets answered

- **Literature** — Read the governing guidance and its rationale for each contested rule (below). Record where guidance is explicit, where it is silent, and where agencies differ.
- **Stakeholder** — Ask technology-readiness-assessment practitioners how the rules are applied in practice, especially where guidance is silent (outreach drafts: NASA oq-1-5, ESA oq-3-3, GAO oq-12-1).

## Research Directions

1. **Composite technologies (2.14).** How do the GAO Technology Readiness Assessment Guide (GAO-20-48G), the NASA TRA best-practices guide (2020), the DoD TRA Guidebook and ISO 16290 / ECSS-E-HB-11A rate a system of components? Is the rating capped by the weakest critical technology element, assessed on the integration, or both?
2. **Scale for TRL 6 (2.12).** What do NPR 7123.1 and the guides say about "relevant environment" and "representative model" when the test article is smaller than the flight article? Are there published scale or fidelity criteria?
3. **Pre-launch level (2.16).** Does any definition require prior spaceflight for TRL 8, or only flight qualification? Record the NASA and ISO wording.
4. **Experiments flown on a mission (2.17).** How do NASA and ESA treat technology demonstration payloads and Class D / in-space demonstration missions at PDR? What waiver and risk-acceptance practice applies?
5. **Amend or interpret (2.18).** Precedent for amending a programme decision versus issuing an interpretation.
6. **Evidence on outcomes.** GAO's annual NASA major-project assessments relate TRL at PDR to cost and schedule growth; extract the effect size.

**Owner decides afterwards:** whether to follow practice where it is explicit, and the stringency where it is silent.
